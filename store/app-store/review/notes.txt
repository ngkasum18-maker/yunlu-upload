App name: 雲路 Yunlu
Bundle ID: com.yunlu.app

This app uploads photos and Word files to the reviewer's / developer's own Yunlu cloud server.

Demo account / delete password: 1014

How to test:
1. Open the app
2. Tap 影相 or 相簿 to upload a photo, or 揀檔案 for a Word document
3. Open an item in the gallery to preview
4. To delete, tap 刪除 and enter password 1014

Privacy policy: https://yunlu-upload.onrender.com/privacy.html
Support: https://yunlu-upload.onrender.com/support.html

No tracking. Camera and photo library are used only when the user taps those buttons.
Export compliance: uses standard HTTPS only (exempt encryption).
