Yunlu is a simple cloud file app for saving photos and Word documents to your own server, then browsing, reading, downloading, or deleting them anytime.

Features
• Take a photo and upload instantly
• Pick multiple photos from the library
• Upload Word (DOC / DOCX) and read DOCX in-app
• Filter the library by all / photos / Word
• Downloads keep original Chinese filenames
• Password-protected delete
• Visitor and preview counts

Files are stored on your deployed Yunlu server, not iCloud. Deploy the backend first so the app can reach your library.
